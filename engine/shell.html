Inside a case

Case text is blurred on this page. Everything else is the real thing.

The patient gets worse while you think

Vitals move on a clock. The nurse tells you when things change. Ask the patient questions in your own words, typed or spoken, and they answer the way a breathless person would: in short bursts.

A debrief that shows what your timing cost

Every critical action you did or missed, when you did it, and why it mattered. Missed the diuretic until the nurse prompted you? It says so.

Pick a patient and go

Cases are grouped by specialty and tagged by level, from medical student to senior resident. Each one takes about ten minutes.

How it works

Talk to the patient

Ask anything. Related details come up on their own, the way patients offer them. Safety questions you still have to ask.

Examine and order

Search hundreds of real orders, or say them out loud. Results come back when they would on shift, not instantly.

Treat under pressure

The right drug helps. The wrong one can hurt. Wait too long and the case moves on without you.

Hand over and learn

Give your handoff and diagnosis, then read a debrief built from what you actually did.
